Planet Carve Privacy Policy

Arbib Games
Effective date: October 5, 2026

This policy describes how Arbib Games (“we”) handles information in the mobile game Planet Carve (package name com.arbibgames.planetcarve). It applies to the Android version distributed on Google Play.

Who we are

Planet Carve is an indie game. We do not run user accounts, a website login, or a player social network. Contact: eyalarbib@gmail.com.

Information we store on your device

Game progress, energy, unlocked ships, purchase unlock flags, and audio settings are stored locally on your device (for example with Android app preferences). This data stays on the device unless you back up or transfer the app. We do not operate our own servers that store your gameplay.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (such as energy refills and ships) are processed by Google Play Billing. Google collects and processes payment details according to Google’s own policies. We receive purchase confirmation needed to unlock the item. We do not receive your full credit card number.

See Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising

Planet Carve shows rewarded ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), which may serve ads from partners including Unity Ads and Google AdMob. Those partners may collect:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID)
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP
  • Ad interaction events (impressions, clicks, rewards)

This data is used to provide, measure, and personalize ads. You can limit ad tracking in Android settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).

Partner policies:

  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • ironSource / LevelPlay Privacy Policy
  • Google / AdMob Privacy Policy

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number inside the game
  • We do not require you to create an account
  • We do not sell your personal information
  • We do not use Unity Analytics or our own crash-reporting service in the current build

Children

Planet Carve is a general-audience game with optional ads and in-app purchases. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child provided such information, contact us and we will delete it where we can.

Your choices

  • Uninstall the app to remove locally stored game data on that device
  • Reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings
  • Manage Google Play purchases and subscriptions in your Google account
  • Contact us to ask what data we hold or to request deletion of anything we control

Full deletion steps: Request deletion of your Planet Carve data

Data safety summary (Google Play)

For Play Console Data safety, Planet Carve:

  • Does not collect account information in-game
  • Shares device identifiers and approximate location with ad partners for advertising
  • Uses purchase data through Google Play to fulfill in-app products
  • Does not encrypt data on independent Arbib Games servers, because we do not host player accounts

Changes

We may update this policy when the game’s features or partners change. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the game after an update means you accept the revised policy.
